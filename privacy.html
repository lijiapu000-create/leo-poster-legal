Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. What LEO poster is

LEO poster is a personal tool, built and operated by an individual, that schedules and publishes the owner's own original travel videos to their own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API. It has a single user: its owner.

2. Data we collect

To function, the Tool processes only what TikTok's OAuth and Content Posting API provide and what you choose to publish:

  • Account identifiers — TikTok open ID and display name, received through TikTok's OAuth flow, used solely to authenticate API calls.
  • OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used solely to publish at your direction.
  • Videos you publish — the video files and captions you select for posting.

These legal pages are static files. They set no cookies and run no analytics or trackers.

3. How data is used

Your data is used for one purpose only: authenticating with TikTok and publishing the videos you choose, when you choose. Nothing else.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own private server with restricted file permissions and are never transmitted except to TikTok's official API endpoints over HTTPS. Videos are processed locally for upload.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. The only external service that receives data is TikTok itself, as required to publish your videos, governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights

You can revoke the Tool's access at any time via your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), which immediately stops all future API calls. To request deletion of stored tokens or data, contact us below and we will delete them promptly.

7. Children

The Tool is not directed at children and is operated solely by its adult owner.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
